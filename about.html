Our Story

Clothes made for
the everyday.

Aabroze started with a simple idea — that the clothes you reach for most often should be the ones that make you feel most like yourself. We design pieces that move between your ordinary Tuesday and your favourite mehfil, without asking you to choose.

HOW IT STARTED

A brand built on the quiet moments in between.

Aabroze was born out of a frustration many women in Pakistan share: closets full of clothes for special occasions, and almost nothing that feels intentional for the everyday. We wanted to close that gap.

Every piece we design starts with the fabric — soft, breathable, and made to hold up to real life — before it ever becomes a print or a silhouette. We work with small local ateliers across Pakistan, keeping our runs limited so quality never gets rushed.

The name Aabroze means "everyday" in Persian-Urdu — a small word that carries the whole idea behind what we make: beauty that doesn't wait for an occasion.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Three things guide every piece we put out.

I.

Fabric first

We choose the textile before the design. If it doesn't feel right against the skin, it doesn't make the cut — no matter how it photographs.

II.

Made close to home

Every piece is cut, stitched, and finished by artisans across Pakistan, in small batches that let us stay close to the details.

III.

Wearable elegance

We design for real bodies and real days — pieces that hold up from the morning commute to a mehfil in the evening.

THE PROCESS

From fabric mill to your doorstep, in small careful batches.

We keep our production runs deliberately small. It means some prints sell out and don't come back — but it also means every piece gets the attention of a workshop, not a factory line.
